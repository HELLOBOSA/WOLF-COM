We look for talent with Nordic rigour and Mediterranean sensibility for an international architecture studio with projects across Spain, Sweden and Greece. Explore our open roles.

Who we are looking for

Architects, interior architects, project managers, site managers, BIM specialists and students looking for an internship. If your work fits Wolfblanc and your role is not one of those, write anyway. We would rather read it and decide ourselves.

  • English, plus at least one of Spanish, Swedish or Greek
  • Working knowledge of BIM, Revit in particular
  • A degree and registration where the role requires it
  • Judgement on drawings and on site, not only software
