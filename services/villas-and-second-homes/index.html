A local architectural lead for your second home.

Wolfblanc helps international clients assess land or property, design new villas and renovations, coordinate permits, consultants and contractors, and stay informed from abroad through structured project reporting.

Horizon House, Galicia

A second home has two briefs

The architectural brief defines the home, its relationship with the site and how it will be used. The delivery brief defines who decides, how progress is reported and how permits, consultants, contractors, quality and handover are controlled when the client is elsewhere.

01

Before purchase

Technical review of a plot or property can identify planning exposure, access, utilities, visible condition, available documentation and likely intervention priorities before commitment.

02

Design for place and use

Architecture, interiors and landscape respond to climate, seasonality, maintenance, privacy, views, daily routines and the way the property will be occupied.

03

Approvals and procurement

Technical documentation, local consultants, permits, contractor selection and offer comparisons are coordinated around the approved scope and target budget.

04

Construction and handover

Site progress, quality, technical questions, changes, budget status, completion inspections, snagging and final documentation are reviewed against the agreed project.

Remote coordination built into the service

Agreed reporting

Site visits and written reports follow an agreed rhythm, covering progress, quality observations, budget status and next decisions.

Clear decision points

Options are presented with their effect on design, cost, programme and maintenance so the client can decide without being on site.

Four working languages

Project communication is available in English, Spanish, Swedish and Greek, helping international clients understand local technical decisions.

One senior lead

A named senior architect remains accountable for the agreed scope, connecting the brief with the decisions made during delivery.

Project monitoring for clients at a distance

Structured reports provide a clear record of site progress, quality observations, open technical questions, budget status and upcoming decisions. The scope and frequency are agreed for each project.

Frequently asked questions

Can you assess a plot before purchase?

Yes. We can provide an initial assessment of planning rules, access, orientation, topography and likely capacity before acquisition progresses.

Can the project be managed while we live abroad?

Yes. We establish responsibilities, a decision calendar, documentation and visual reporting so distance does not reduce control.

Do you include interiors and outdoor spaces?

We can integrate architecture, interiors and landscape when that continuity is important to the project.
